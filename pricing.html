Ask about membership prices

Contact the academy for the price of your chosen group. The available schedule posts do not include a price list. Before buying a membership, confirm the sport, branch, age group and how many classes it covers.

Also ask whether a trial class has a separate fee and whether you need to buy a uniform before attending. Do not assume that a student discount, a free trial or an unlimited membership is available.

Questions before paying

  1. What is the price for my age group and sport?
  2. How many classes does the membership include?
  3. When does the membership start and expire?
  4. What happens if I miss a class?
  5. Are equipment or uniform costs separate?
  6. Which payment methods are accepted?

Ask about cancellations and missed classes before making a payment. The academy can explain its current terms. This site does not take payments or issue memberships.
