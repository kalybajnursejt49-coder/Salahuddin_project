Official Rates and Pass Tiers

We maintain clear and fixed pricing with zero registration surcharges. Every pass grants full access to locker rooms, individual lockers, hot showers, filtered water stations, and intro safety briefings. Pass holders can request a temporary freeze in case of illness, travel, or university examination periods.

Gear and Equipment Guidelines

Mandatory Safety Equipment

  1. Form-fitted dental mouthguard.
  2. Cotton boxing hand wraps (minimum 3.5 meters length).
  3. Groin protection guard for sparring participants.
  4. Padded shin and instep guards.
