Meet our coaches and explore their classes.

Raimbek Tomataev

Raimbek is the founder of Salahuddin Academy. He has a second dan black belt and holds the title of candidate for the Master of Sports of Kazakhstan. He has seven years of coaching experience. He also coached champions of Kazakhstan, Eurasia and the world.

Arlan Amanzhol

Arlan trains taekwondo athletes. He holds the title of candidate master of sports, has won international competitions, and has four years of coaching experience. He is also a medalist of the Confederation Cup and the Universiade.

Aisunkar Donenbaev

Aisunkar is a belt wrestling master of sports and a candidate for Master of Sports in judo, sambo and Kazakh wrestling. He is the champion of Kazakhstan in belt wrestling. He has four years of coaching experience.

Olzhas Mukhamedgaliyev

Olzhas is a certified Thai boxing coach and a Master of Sports of Kazakhstan. He has 9 years of coaching experience and also trained at Phuket Fight Club in Thailand in 2014, 2015, and 2022.
