Head Karate Instructor: Salauat Ibragimov

With over twelve years of continuous coaching experience, Salauat has trained national cup champions and medalists across Kazakhstan. His instructional methodology focuses on technical precision, mental poise under pressure, and developing cardiovascular stamina required for full-contact combat.

Master Salauat regularly stresses: A belt only holds your uniform together; your true standard is proven by your humility and work ethic in the dojo.

Head Boxing Coach: Daniyar Ospanov

Daniyar is a graduate of the Kazakh Academy of Sports and Tourism and an accomplished regional champion. He specializes in teaching clean punching mechanics to beginners while preparing competitive juniors for regional amateur tournaments through footwork drills and situational sparring.

Grading and Belt Promotion Criteria

  1. Demonstration of stationary and advancing basic techniques (Kihon).
  2. Execution of formal movement patterns and sequences (Kata / Poomsae).
  3. Physical fitness benchmark testing (continuous push-ups, squats, and jumps).
  4. Consecutive full-contact endurance rounds (Kumite) against rotating opponents.
