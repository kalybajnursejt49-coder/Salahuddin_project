These coach details come from the academy's posts dated 3 September 2025. Experience is shown as it was listed in those posts.

Raimbek Tomataev

Raimbek is the founder of Salahuddin Academy. He has a second dan black belt., the title of candidate for the Master of Sports of Kazakhstan and seven years of coaching experience. He also coached the champions of Kazakhstan, Eurasia and the world.

Arlan Amanzhol

Arlan trains taekwondo athletes.He holds the title of candidate master of sports, has won international competitions, and has four years of coaching experience. He is also a medalist of the Confederation Cup and the Universiade.

Aisunkar Donenbaev

Aisunkar is a belt wrestling master of sports and a candidate for Master of Sports in judo, sambo and Kazakh wrestling. He is the champion of Kazakhstan in belt wrestling. With four years of experience

Olzhas Mukhamedgaliyev

Olzhas is a certified Thai boxing coach and a Master of Sports of Kazakhstan. He has 9 years of coaching experience and also trained at Phuket Fight Club in Thailand in 2014, 2015, and 2022.
