About Our Academy

Salahuddin Martial Arts Academy is located in the Yesil district of Astana at 55 Uly Dala Avenue. Since its establishment, our mission has been to prepare skilled athletes while fostering discipline, mental fortitude, and a healthy lifestyle among youth and adults. We bring together strike-based disciplines and classic combat systems under one roof.

Our facility occupies the basement floor of a residential building and is equipped with professional shock-absorbing tatami mats, heavy punching bags, wall-mounted pads, and strength conditioning gear. We offer both group sessions and individualized one-on-one coaching led by experienced instructors.

Dojo Culture and Training Process

Code of Conduct for Fighters

  1. Perform a respectful bow (rei) when entering and leaving the dojo floor.
  2. Arrive on time prior to the start of the scheduled session.
  3. Maintain a clean and tidy uniform (gi, dobok, or athletic combat gear).

Athlete Preparation Stages

  • Physical Conditioning
    • Joint warmup and dynamic stretching
    • Core strength and aerobic conditioning
  • Technical Foundations
    • Stances, footwork, and balance control
    • Defensive blocks and targeted striking combinations
  • Controlled Sparring in Protective Equipment

Glossary of Martial Arts Terms

Kyokushin
A full-contact stand-up karate discipline founded by Masutatsu Oyama in the mid-twentieth century.
Dojo
A dedicated space used for physical training, moral discipline, and personal development.
Tatami
A high-density interlocking foam mat system engineered to cushion impacts and prevent joint injuries.

Weight Divisions and Facility Rules

Official weight divisions for youth and adult sparring tournaments:

  • Flyweight: up to 55 kg
  • Lightweight: 55 kg to 65 kg
  • Middleweight: 65 kg to 75 kg
  • Heavyweight: over 75 kg

Our training area covers 120 square meters and features certified impact-absorbing tatami mats. Athletes must remove street shoes before stepping onto the mat area.
