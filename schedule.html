Weekly Training Timetable

Our schedule balances time slots so junior and senior groups have full, safe access to the tatami mat surface. Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays focus on Kyokushin karate and boxing drills, while Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays host Olympic taekwondo technical work, youth fundamentals, and open ring sparring.
