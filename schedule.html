Before choosing a group

Start with the address, then check the sport, age range and time. Some sports have both morning and evening groups. Saturday times can differ from weekday times. An age written with a plus sign means that age and older.

The Uly Dala karate poster lists some overlapping times. They are copied as published; ask the academy which group you should attend. The posters do not name a coach for every group, so those names have not been guessed.

These posts do not tell us whether places are still available or whether a group has moved. Use the contact page to check before your first visit.
