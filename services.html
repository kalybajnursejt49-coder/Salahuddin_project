Sports at Salahuddin

The academy's posts list four sports: Kyokushinkai karate, taekwondo, judo and Muay Thai. The available groups depend on the branch and the student's age.

Kyokushinkai karate
The Uly Dala schedule includes groups from age four and a group for ages thirteen and older.
Taekwondo
The Uly Dala poster lists groups for ages six to sixteen.
Judo
The Uly Dala poster lists weekday evening classes and a Saturday class for ages seven to sixteen.
Muay Thai
The Uly Dala posters show a children's group and a separate group for adults aged eighteen and older.

Check the schedule for the exact days and times.

Before choosing a class, ask which group has spaces and whether beginners can join it. Also ask what clothes or equipment are needed for the first session. Confirm the trial price before visiting; the supplied posters do not give one.
